# JolSathi · JolBank

JolSathi is the bilingual (Bangla–English) app of **JolBank**, a system that keeps drinking water from the managed aquifer recharge (MAR) sites of Khulna, Satkhira and Bagerhat tested, protected and fairly shared.

**Live demo:** https://tabassummim.github.io/jolsathi/

## What the demo shows
- **Family:** today's water status (green, yellow, red), tests behind it, weekly water share, jar orders, where the money goes, and the SMS sent to families without smartphones.
- **Caretaker:** jobs generated from test results, weekly tests (salt, cloudiness, germ vial, arsenic kit, meters), work proof with photo, offline saving.
- **Supervisor:** verify work and pay, enter laboratory results, register households up to each site's safe capacity.
- **Vendor:** jar deliveries and the Tk 3 levy to the hub fund.
- **Hub dashboard:** all sites, alerts, minimum-service mode and income shares.

Tap the **online/offline** badge to show how records wait as "pending" and send when the signal returns.

## Notes
Prototype with demonstration data. All data is stored only in the visitor's browser; "Reset demo" restores the starting data.
